About ZELOSBIA

Small, serious, and responsive

ZELOSBIA Trading and Sourcing is a Batam based trading and sourcing business focused on K3 safety equipment, industrial supplies, construction site requirements, and general operational needs.

We support businesses and procurement teams by helping identify suitable products and supplier options based on required specifications, quantity, budget, and delivery requirements.

Our approach is practical and straightforward. We understand the requirement, identify suitable sourcing options, coordinate quality checking when required, and support the logistics process.
